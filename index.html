01

Welcome & introduction

Welcome to Planaria Studio. We are glad you are here.

Planaria started as an idea shared between a few creatives who believed Indonesian talent belonged alongside the best in the world. Late nights and modest gigs grew into a team of skilled, dependable people doing world-class post-production for global clients.

This handbook sets out how we work, what we expect, and what you can expect from us. Our policies rest on trust, mutual respect, and the belief that a sustainable creative career is possible here.

We're building something meaningful here, and we're glad to have you as part of it.

The Leadership Team, Planaria Studio

02

Company mission, vision & values

Our mission is to deliver the standard of post-production expected from leading studios in the U.S. and Europe, from Indonesia.

World-class output should not carry a prohibitive price tag. Affordable, high-quality work for global clients creates fairly paid opportunities for Indonesian creatives.

Our values:

  • Quality. We hold ourselves to an international standard, every time.
  • Integrity. We act with honesty toward our clients, our partners, and each other.
  • Reliability. We do what we say we will do, and we do it well.
  • Sustainability. We build careers, not just projects.
  • Respect. We treat everyone, teammates, clients and partners alike, with professionalism and care.
03

Company story

Planaria Studio started very small. In 2022 we were a couple of editors splitting modest jobs on educational content, working the business out as we went.

What began as a side project quickly became something more serious. Deadlines grew, projects became more complex, and demand for our work steadily increased. By 2026, we had grown into a 13-person studio with a structured workflow, a defined creative process, and a clear shared vision.

We're based in Indonesia, but our clients and our mindset are global. Through all the growth and challenges, one principle has remained constant:

"We believe in doing great work with good people, and building something sustainable together."

04

What this handbook is & isn't

This is your reference for how the studio operates: values, policies, expectations, and the support available to you.

That said, it is important to understand what this handbook is not:

  • It is not a contract. Your individual employment contract is the binding document governing your specific terms. Where this handbook and your contract differ, your contract takes precedence.
  • It is not a rigid rulebook. We operate in a fast-moving, creative industry. Sometimes we adapt faster than this document can be updated.
  • It is not a micromanagement tool. We trust you. The policies here exist to protect the team and create the conditions for everyone to do their best work.

If anything here is unclear, outdated, or does not match how we actually operate, raise it with management.

05

Employment policy

Equal opportunity employment

Planaria Studio is an equal opportunity employer. Hiring, pay, promotion and every other employment decision rests on skills, experience and professional conduct. Not race, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or any other personal characteristic.

We are committed to a fair and inclusive workplace where everyone is respected and supported.

Anti-harassment & discrimination policy

Every member of the team deserves to feel safe and respected at work, without exception.

We do not tolerate harassment, bullying or discrimination in any form, verbal, physical, visual or digital. That includes inappropriate remarks, unwanted advances, offensive commentary, and anything that causes discomfort or exclusion.

Report it immediately to a team lead or to management. Every report is taken seriously and handled confidentially. Retaliation against anyone raising a concern in good faith is prohibited.

Code of conduct & ethics

Professional conduct is the foundation of everything we do. As a member of the team, we expect you to:

  • Communicate honestly, clearly, and professionally
  • Meet your deadlines and take full ownership of your work
  • Treat teammates, clients, and partners with respect
  • Represent the studio in a manner consistent with our values
  • Keep personal conflicts separate from professional responsibilities

Act with integrity in every interaction, whether or not anyone is watching.

Confidentiality & non-disclosure

Clients share footage, scripts, brand assets, creative direction and sensitive information with us. Protecting it is our responsibility.

You agree not to share, repurpose or disclose confidential materials or communications, during your employment or after it ends. That covers project files, client assets, internal workflows, financial information and any other proprietary studio content.

When in doubt

Assume it is confidential, and ask before sharing.

06

Working at Planaria Studio

6.1 Work environment & etiquette

We are a creative, fast-paced team. We work best when the environment is focused, supportive, and professional.

We expect everyone to:

  • Communicate clearly and respond in a timely manner
  • Be reliable. Others depend on your contribution
  • Keep your workspace, physical or digital, organized
  • Maintain professionalism, even under pressure
  • Ask for clarification when you need it. There is no shame in it

We do not operate through rigid hierarchy, but we do expect consistent professionalism from everyone.

6.2 Logging attendance

Every team member is required to log their attendance daily through GoodDay.

Where to log: All attendance entries go through Staff Hub → Attendance, on one of two tabs. On a working day you fill in Daily Standup. On a day you are not working you fill in Time Off Request instead, and never both. These are the only accepted methods. Anything sent by chat, email or message will not be recorded.

When to log: Fill in the standup every working day, without exception. On days off, submit a Time Off Request at least 2 working days in advance. This serves as a formal request to management. A notification will be sent confirming approval or rejection, along with the reason.

The step by step version

Every screen, with where to click, is in Attendance & Compensation Policy. What follows here is the rule.

How to fill in the standup, each field explained:

FieldWhat goes in it
Your namePick yourself from the list. This is what files the day against you, so choose your own name even if you are on somebody else's screen
Which job?The job name exactly as it appears on the Projects board. It becomes the title of the record, so it is what makes the day findable later

Why it matters: Attendance data is used for three purposes: official presence records, workload visibility for your lead, and capacity planning across the team.

One field is gone

The old Attendance & Workload Status field (Heavy, Medium, Light, Standby, OFF) no longer exists. Your lead now reads how loaded you are from the jobs on the board and from what you write in the standup, not from a rating you give yourself. Days off are handled by the Time Off Request tab instead of by an OFF status.

6.3 Monthly attendance cut-off

Attendance runs on a fixed monthly cycle with a hard deadline.

Each attendance period runs from the 26th of the previous month through the 25th of the current month. All entries and corrections must be finalized by the 25th at 23:59 WIB.

There is no catching up later

Incomplete records after cut-off will be treated as unrecorded. There is no mechanism for retroactive corrections. Any data not submitted by the 25th will not be processed.

Self-check before the deadline: Before the 25th, verify that no dates are missing, all statuses are accurate, and notes are filled in with sufficient detail.

How this ties to payroll: Salaries are processed at the end of each month based on the attendance data locked at cut-off. Your pay slip, including any attendance adjustments, is sent to your email.

6.4 Working hours & overtime

Planaria Studio operates on a flexible work arrangement. Output and deadlines take priority over fixed schedules.

Standard working hours: The expected working arrangement averages 8 effective hours per day or 40 hours per week, excluding breaks. That follows Indonesian labour regulations (the Manpower Law (UU No. 13/2003), as amended) and is a reference point, not a rigid daily target.

Standby availability: Because most of our clients are based overseas, team members must remain available and responsive during core collaboration windows. Standard standby hours are:

  • 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM (WIB)
  • 6:00 PM – 10:00 PM (WIB)

During these windows you are expected to be reachable, alert, and capable of responding to feedback or making quick revisions when required.

Note: Some roles operate under different standby schedules as specified in their individual contracts. Please refer to your contract for the hours applicable to your position.

Overtime: Overtime work must be based on mutual agreement and is subject to applicable legal limits under Indonesian law. If your workload consistently exceeds reasonable limits, you are entitled to raise this formally. Submit supporting evidence to your lead and management: task logs, project timelines, or communication records. Overtime reporting serves as operational data to help the team rebalance workloads, adjust deadlines, or allocate additional resources.

Note: Statutory overtime entitlements for employees on permanent or fixed-term contracts are governed by Indonesian labour law and your individual contract. The studio's obligations in this regard are non-negotiable and will be honored in full.

6.5 Remote work policy

Planaria Studio is a remote-first team. Whether you work from home, a coworking space, or elsewhere, the expectations remain the same: deliver quality work, communicate openly, and meet your deadlines.

For team calls: be on time, be in a quiet environment, and be fully present.

If we ever transition to a hybrid or in-office format, you will be given clear advance notice and support through the change.

6.6 Remote work setup requirements

Working remotely means taking full ownership of your own setup. To perform at the level we expect, you should have:

  • A stable, reliable internet connection
  • A quiet, distraction-free workspace
  • A functioning computer capable of handling our production workflow
  • Access to all required software and tools (licenses will be provided by the studio where applicable)

If your setup becomes a barrier to your work, raise it with management. We want to help find a solution.

Time off & leave

Planaria Studio operates across all seven days of the week. The following policies are designed to protect your rest, health, and legal entitlements while keeping the team functioning smoothly.

All time-off requests must be submitted through the Time Off Request tab in Staff Hub → Attendance at least 2 working days in advance, unless stated otherwise below.

The step by step version

How to actually file one, and what each answer means, is in Attendance & Compensation Policy. This section is what you are entitled to.

7.1 Day off (monthly rest entitlement)

Sunday is a fixed day off for everyone. You do not request it and you do not log it.

Every employee receives 8 days off per month. Sundays count against that 8. The remainder are yours to request. No employee takes more than 9 days off in any one month. This allocation is separate from your annual leave.

Month withSundays, automaticLeft to requestCeiling
4 Sundays449
5 Sundays539

The ninth day sits above the normal 8 so a rolled-over day can still be used. It is a ceiling, not an entitlement.

How to request the rest: open the Time Off Request tab, choose Day off, set the day. For several days in a row, set the first and the last. Give the reason in Anything Julius should know?, the only place he can see one. You get a notification with the answer and the reason.

If called in on a day off: where the work runs past 2 effective hours, you are entitled to either a replacement day at an agreed time, or monetary compensation as defined in your contract. This covers Sundays.

Rollover, the N+2 window: unused days off do not expire at the end of the month. They carry forward on this schedule:

PeriodStatus
Month NDays off earned but not taken
Month N+1Still available as regular days off
Month N+2Final window to take remaining days
After N+2Automatically converted to monetary compensation and included in payroll

Note: The monetary conversion rate for unused days off after the N+2 window is specified in your individual contract.

7.2 Annual leave, government-mandated

In addition to monthly days off, all employees are entitled to government-mandated annual leave under Indonesian labour law (UU No. 13/2003, Article 79, as amended by UU No. 6/2023).

Entitlement:

  • Employees who have completed 12 months of continuous service are entitled to a minimum of 12 working days of paid annual leave per year
  • This entitlement accrues at 1 day per month of completed service
  • Employees with less than 12 months of service are not yet entitled to the full annual leave allocation; accrual begins from the first month of employment

How to request: Submit through the Time Off Request tab at least 2 working days in advance, following the same process as day-off requests.

Paid leave is not automatic

A request only becomes paid leave when Julius marks it as PAID LEAVE, and payroll reads that. If you believe a day should be paid leave, say so in the request. Do not assume it.

Consecutive day limit: Leave may be taken for up to 4 consecutive days at a time. Following that, the employee is required to work at least 5 full working days before submitting another leave request.

Annual leave cannot be cashed out

Paid annual leave cannot be cashed out under any circumstances. Monthly days off are compensated automatically after the N+2 window. Unused annual leave is not, and expires without conversion. This is consistent with current Indonesian labour regulations.

Sick leave and annual leave: Absence due to illness or injury does not count against your annual leave balance, provided you notify your direct lead in a timely manner and provide supporting documentation as required. This is your right under Indonesian labour law.

Note: Your individual contract specifies the full breakdown of your leave entitlements. Please refer to your contract for complete details.

7.3 Unplanned absence & sick leave

In all cases of unplanned absence, you are expected to notify your direct lead (Supervisor) or management at the earliest opportunity, including in last-minute situations.

Deduction order for personal absences: For unplanned absences outside of scheduled time off, available balances are deducted in the following order:

  1. Paid annual leave, deducted first
  2. Monthly days off, deducted if annual leave is exhausted
  3. Unpaid leave, applied as a last resort if both balances are exhausted

Sick leave: Being sick will not affect your pay, as long as you notify your direct lead in a timely manner and provide supporting documentation as required by company policy. Sick leave does not reduce your annual leave or day-off balance. File it the same morning on the Time Off Request tab, choose Sick, and attach the doctor's note to the request if you have one.

Emergency absence: In all emergency situations, notify your direct lead as soon as reasonably possible. Documentation may be required upon return.

Extended absence (3+ consecutive working days): Absences exceeding 3 consecutive working days, or those arising from special circumstances, are handled on a case-by-case basis and require approval from both your direct lead (Supervisor) and management.

Compensation & performance

8.1 Payroll & salary

Salaries at Planaria Studio are set based on your role, scope of responsibilities, and contribution to the team. We aim to offer competitive rates reflective of the international standard of work we deliver.

Any change to your compensation, including promotions, raises and structural adjustments, is communicated in writing. We are transparent about pay. If you ever have questions about your salary or how it is structured, reach out to management directly.

8.2 Pay schedule & salary restructuring

All team members are paid on a monthly basis. Salaries are processed at the end of each month based on attendance data locked at the 25th cut-off. Your pay slip will be sent to your registered email.

Please ensure your banking details on file are accurate and up to date. If there is ever a delay or discrepancy, notify management immediately.

Effective March 2026, salary restructuring: Two structural changes apply to all employees:

  1. Income tax (income tax) withholding is applied in accordance with applicable Indonesian tax law. Under the average effective rate system effective since January 2024, monthly withholding is applied from January through November, with a full progressive reconciliation in December.
  2. Restructuring of salary components. The breakdown of each employee's compensation has been adjusted by role. A personalized breakdown with the full details of your updated compensation structure will be sent to your registered email. Please review and confirm receipt.

If you have questions about your compensation structure, reach out to your direct lead or management.

8.3 Overtime compensation

Planaria Studio operates on a flexible, output-based work model. We trust you to manage your time responsibly.

However, we recognize that certain projects will demand more than the standard workload. Under Indonesian labour law (UU No. 13/2003, Article 78), overtime work must be based on employee consent and is subject to legal limits. Statutory overtime entitlements for employees under formal employment agreements are defined in your individual contract and will be honored in full.

If your workload consistently exceeds reasonable limits, you are entitled to raise this formally by submitting supporting evidence to your lead and management. That triggers a review of workload, deadlines and resources. Not punishment.

We are committed to a sustainable working environment for everyone on the team.

8.4 Religious holiday allowance

All employees at Planaria Studio are entitled to the religious holiday allowance as mandated under Government Regulation No. 36/2021 and Minister of Manpower Regulation No. 6/2016.

Who is eligible: All employees who have completed at least 1 month of continuous service, whether you are on a permanent or a fixed-term contract.

Calculation:

Length of serviceWhat you receive
12 or more months of continuous serviceOne full month's salary
Less than 12 months of continuous serviceProrated: (months of service ÷ 12) × one month's salary

Payment deadline: the holiday allowance must be paid no later than 7 days before the employee's primary religious holiday. Payment is made in a single lump sum, installment payments are not permitted.

the holiday allowance is paid according to the employee's registered religion. Indonesia officially recognizes six religions (Islam, Protestantism, Catholicism, Hinduism, Buddhism, and Confucianism), each with its corresponding primary holiday.

Tax treatment: the holiday allowance is subject to income tax income tax withholding under applicable Indonesian tax law. It is not subject to BPJS contributions.

Late payment carries a statutory fine

A late holiday allowance payment is subject to a statutory fine of 5% of the total allowance owed, per Government Regulation No. 36/2021. Planaria Studio is committed to full and timely compliance.

8.5 Performance reviews & feedback

We run bi-annual performance reviews to:

  • Check in on individual goals and progress
  • Provide structured, constructive feedback
  • Discuss what is working well and what needs improvement
  • Address compensation changes, promotions, or role adjustments

Reviews are announced in advance and run both ways. Your view of how the company is operating carries the same weight.

Between formal reviews, you will receive informal feedback as projects conclude. We do not do surprises. Just consistent, open communication.

Formal warnings (SP1, SP2) issued during any period will be referenced in the relevant bi-annual review.

09

Conduct, disciplinary actions & grievances

What constitutes misconduct

We expect every team member to act with professionalism, honesty and respect, in how they work and how they treat others.

Misconduct includes, but is not limited to:

  • Consistent failure to meet deadlines or deliver assigned work
  • Ignoring client or team communication without valid reason
  • Verbal abuse, harassment, or discriminatory behavior of any kind
  • Sexual harassment or any conduct of a sexual nature that causes discomfort, harm, or a hostile work environment
  • Sharing confidential materials or client files without authorization
  • Misuse of company tools, platforms, or resources
  • Dishonesty or misrepresentation in any form

Mistakes happen. Misconduct becomes a formal matter when it is repeated, intentional, or harms the team, a client, or the company's reputation.

Disciplinary process

Any concern about conduct or performance starts with a direct conversation. The goal is resolution, not punishment.

The standard disciplinary process follows these steps:

1

Verbal reminder

An informal discussion to identify and address the issue. No formal record is created at this stage, but the conversation will be noted internally.

2

Written warning (SP1)

A formal written notice issued if the issue persists after a verbal reminder, or if the initial infraction warrants immediate written documentation. SP1 carries no financial sanction.

3

Final warning (SP2)

Issued if the behavior or performance issue continues after SP1, or if a subsequent violation occurs within the SP1 active period. SP2 carries a formal financial sanction in addition to the written warning:

As provided under these Company Regulations, and in accordance with PP No. 36/2021 Article 20, the issuance of SP2 may result in the partial or full withholding of variable allowances, meaning performance incentives and other non-fixed pay components for a defined period as determined by management. Base salary is not affected. The total deduction across all components shall not exceed 50% of the employee's total monthly compensation in any given pay period.

The specific allowance components covered by this are defined in the employee's individual contract or a separate written amendment communicated at the time of SP2 issuance.

4

Termination (SP3)

Issued if all prior steps fail to produce the required improvement, or if the misconduct is of sufficient severity to warrant immediate termination. SP3 constitutes notice of termination of employment.

Each formal warning (SP1, SP2) remains active for two quarters (6 months) from the date of issuance and will be referenced in the relevant bi-annual performance review.

Note: The steps above represent the standard progression. Management may issue any level of warning, including SP2 or SP3 directly, based on the nature and severity of the misconduct, without following the full sequence.

Discretionary suspension

In addition to the standard disciplinary process above, management may impose a suspension at its discretion at any stage, including before any SP has been issued, when circumstances require it.

Suspension is not a mandatory step in the disciplinary process. It is a separate measure available to management and may be applied independently of or alongside any SP.

When suspension may be applied: Suspension is appropriate when the employee's continued presence in the work environment poses a risk to the team, the investigation process, client relationships, or the integrity of the workplace. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Credible allegations of sexual harassment or assault
  • Credible allegations of physical violence or threats
  • Serious breach of confidentiality under active investigation
  • Any situation where the employee's presence may compromise a fair investigation

Terms of suspension:

  • Duration is as determined by management, based on the nature and complexity of the situation
  • During suspension, the employee's base salary continues to be paid in full, as required under UU No. 13/2003 Article 155
  • Variable allowances may be withheld during the suspension, as provided under these Company Regulations
  • The employee will be notified in writing of the suspension, its reason, its expected duration, and the investigation process
  • The employee is expected to remain reachable and to cooperate fully with any investigation during the suspension period

Following the investigation: Depending on the outcome, the suspension may result in reinstatement with no further action, issuance of an appropriate SP, or termination (SP3) where warranted by the findings.

Unpaid suspension is never used

Imposing suspension without pay, or failing to pay base salary during a suspension, constitutes a violation of Indonesian labour law and exposes the company to legal liability. Planaria Studio will not do this.

Reporting violations or grievances

If you experience or witness behaviour that crosses a line, or feel you have been treated unfairly, we want to know.

Reports can be directed to Rashy Azhardwian. All reports will be handled with full confidentiality and taken seriously. For reports involving sexual harassment specifically, the matter will be treated as a priority and may trigger an immediate suspension of the accused party pending investigation.

There is no retaliation for raising a concern in good faith. We would rather deal with something directly than let it fester. Everyone here deserves a safe, respectful and fair place to work.

10

Leaving the company

Resignation procedure

If you decide to move on, we ask for a minimum of 30 days' written notice, submitted by email or through GoodDay.

This allows us to plan for a smooth handover for the team and our clients. During your notice period, please continue to fulfill your responsibilities and assist with the transition of your tasks.

Exit interviews

Prior to your last day, we will schedule a brief exit interview. Your chance to say candidly what worked, what did not, and where we can improve.

Final paycheck & benefits

Your final paycheck will include all outstanding salary, any unused day-off entitlements subject to monetary conversion (per rollover rules in Section 7.1), and any other owed benefits as specified in your contract.

It will be processed on the next regular pay cycle following your last working day. If you have questions about your final pay, contact management directly.

Return of company property

Please return all company property before your final day. That includes, but is not limited to, equipment, software licences, project files, and any other materials related to your work here.

If you are unsure what needs to be returned, confirm with your manager in advance of your last day.

Grounds for involuntary termination

Involuntary termination may occur as a result of:

  • Serious or repeated misconduct
  • Consistent failure to meet performance expectations
  • Breach of confidentiality or company policy
  • Actions that significantly harm the team, clients, or the business

Every termination is handled fairly and in compliance with Indonesian labour law. You will be told the reasons and given the chance to ask questions.
